The register of areas

Six, and no seventh
01

Run

The daily business

Customers, quotes and invoices, payments matched from the bank feed, VAT prepared for signature, inventory that reserves itself against orders, jobs on a schedule, and support threads attached to the customer they belong to.

02

Grow

Demand and clients

A pipeline that reads from the same customer record as your invoices, a client portal where proposals are read and signed, marketing lists that respect who has already bought, and a forecast built from what is actually committed.

03

Work

Execution and approvals

Boards for the work in front of you, planning for the weeks after it, and one approval queue that every other area raises into — so a held payment and a disputed line arrive in the same place, with the same deadline.

04

Automate

The engine

A workflow engine with connectors to the systems you already pay for, monitoring that tells you what ran and what was held, and AI-assisted mapping that proposes how an incoming document becomes an order — then waits for you.

05

Reports

The account of record

Dashboards for the day, custom tables for the questions nobody anticipated, and compliance exports that carry their own provenance: which definition, which refresh, whose signature, and when.

06

Settings

Authority and access

Billing you can read, roles that map to the six areas rather than to a list of switches, security defaults that start closed, and API access for the parts of your business we have not met yet.

The interface

The whole business on one page, before you touch anything

Omnia opens on the state of the company: what came in, what is owed, what is due to the tax authority, and the short list of decisions only you can make. Nothing is buried behind a module, and nothing acts without your word.
